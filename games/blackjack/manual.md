# How to play
Beat the dealer by getting closer to twenty-one without going over.

Number cards are face value, picture cards are ten, and an ace is eleven
or one — whichever helps you.

# The house
Six decks, reshuffled when a quarter remains. The dealer stands on soft
seventeen. Blackjack pays three to two.

# Your options
  hit      take another card
  stand    keep what you have
  double   double your bet and take exactly one more card
  split    a pair becomes two hands, each with its own bet

Insurance is offered when the dealer shows an ace. It pays two to one if
they have blackjack, and loses otherwise.

# Chips
Your chips are shared with every other game on the console, so what you
win here you carry with you.
