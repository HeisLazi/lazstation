# HAVEN - a story mode for Cataclysm

## What am I playing for?

One sentence: **reach Haven, the settlement that is still lit far to the
east - and retire your survivor into the postgame.**

Vanilla Cataclysm is endless survival with no finish line. Haven Story adds
one: a radio loop on 91.1, a dead courier with a chit stamped with next
month's date, and a marked road east. Everything you do - water, trust,
bullets, engines - points at the gate. Walk through it and the run ends:
your survivor settles in Haven, the work board opens with repeatable
contracts, and the world stays yours as a free sandbox. That arrival is the
victory screen this game never had.

Full run, once all acts ship: **10+ hours.** v1 (this release) is the
**Intro + Act 1 (~2-3 hours)**, with the rest of the spine already written
so every early line pays off later.

## How to play it

1. `lazstation play cataclysm` (or run the game directly).
2. Create (or load) the **HavenStory** world - it ships with the mod
   enabled. If you make your own world, enable **Haven Story** in the mod
   list.
3. Pick the **Haven Bound (Story)** scenario. You start in an evac shelter
   with the intro mission already in your log (`m` to read missions).
4. Survive day 1, read the flyer, then find living people and ask for work.

## The storyline

### INTRO - The Voice on 91.1 (you, day 1)
The shelter radio loops four words all dusk: *Haven is lit. The light stays
on.* Outside lies a courier, dead a week, clutching a Haven chit stamped
with a date that hasn't happened yet. Live through the first day. Then go
east the only way anyone goes anywhere now: by being useful.

### ACT 1 - Dust & Barter (implemented in v1)
The crossroads around your shelter. Three jobs that unlock each other, plus
two optional sides. All optional in the sandbox sense - but each opens a
route the others need:

- **Q1 - Water for Old Tom** (fetch, no combat): one clean water. Pays in
  Tom's chit + his word. *Talking to Tom and doing X is what unlocks Y:*
  Micah won't deal without Tom's vouch.
- **Q2 - Micah's Price** (combat, 6 zombies): clear the salvage yard. Pays
  in a working **car alternator** - the `B` someone else needs.
- **Q3 - Light the Tower** (repair/finale): fit the alternator for Lena,
  the tower lights, 91.1 carries for miles. Pays in **Lena's marked road
  map** - your Act 2 ticket. *B fixed C for a whole different quest.*
- **Side - Hounds in the Cellar** (hard combat, 3 zombie dogs): pays in a
  machete. The gear route - makes Q2 much easier.
- **Side - The Tinker's Debt** (fetch, scrap metal): pays in a *spare*
  alternator. The quiet route - skip Micah entirely and still light the
  tower. Two roads, one light.

Closing Q4 (`The Long Road Ahead`) banks Act 1 and briefs you on the rest.

### Side arcs - the wild rides (Arc A implemented, B + C planned)
Full plans + dialogue highlights: `SIDEQUESTS.md`. The rule: 30+ minutes
each, a different flavor every time, dialogue doing the heavy lifting,
and loot - not payouts - as the real reward.

- **A - The Cure That Bites** (heist-horror, playable now): the Ash
  Jackals, Dr. Morrow's miracle cure, a clinic raid, and the red folder
  that ruins everything. Ends in a real choice: storm the lab alone
  (near-suicide vs his prize brute) or bring the gang (fire + witnesses).
- **B - The Toll at Mile 12** (heist-comedy, planned): four solutions to
  one toll booth - pay, race, con, or fight.
- **C - The Silo That Hums** (quiet horror, planned): a silo receiving
  91.1 - and something else listening. Direct prequel to Act 4.

### ACT 2 - The Long Road (planned)
Fuel, a running car, burnt towns. Escort and delivery jobs; your driving
and mechanics decide which route survives. Ends with headlights on Glass
City.

### ACT 3 - Glass City (planned)
A hospital computer holds outbreak records. Moral fork: carry out medicine
for the living, or answers for the future. Your choice changes who meets
you in Act 5.

### ACT 4 - Static (planned)
The broadcast lies - or loops. The courier's too-late chit finally makes
sense. This act reframes the intro: you replay day 1 knowing what 91.1 is.

### ACT 5 - Haven Gate (planned)
Three ways in: fight through the toll gangs, sneak the culverts, or pay
the gate price you spent four acts earning. The guard greets you with the
intro's four words. Then the gate opens.

### FINALE - Haven (planned) + postgame
Arrival. A bed, a wall, a work board with repeatable contracts, and the
full sandbox with your built-up survivor. The run is won; the world is
yours.

## Balance notes (v1)

Act 1 is deliberately gentle: difficulty 1 fetch, difficulty 2 yard fight,
difficulty 3 optional hounds, two fetch alternates. Nothing timed, nothing
missable. The 10-hour arc comes from the full chain (vehicle play, skills,
distance) in Acts 2-5, not from grinding Act 1.
