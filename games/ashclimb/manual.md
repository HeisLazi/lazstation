# How to play
Twelve floors. You climb until you reach the Cinder King, or you fall.

Each turn you are dealt a hand and given three energy. Play cards by
number, and press 0 when you are done. Unspent energy is lost, and your
hand is discarded at the end of every turn.

# Reading a fight
Every enemy shows its next move before you commit — "attack 11", "block 6".
That telegraph is the whole game: block when a big hit is coming, press
when it is defending.

Block is spent absorbing damage and clears at the start of your next turn.
Vulnerable makes a target take half again as much. Weak makes it deal a
quarter less.

# The deck
Every card you take makes the deck stronger and slower. Ten good cards
beat thirty mixed ones, so skipping a reward is a real move. Rest sites
let you burn a card out of the deck for good.

# Controls
  1-5     play that card
  0       end your turn
  number  choose a target when there is more than one enemy
