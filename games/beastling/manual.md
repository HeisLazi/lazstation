# How to play
Catch beasts, raise them, and beat the five champions who keep the roads.

You start with one beast and eight lures. Travel to a route, walk through
the grass, and fight whatever finds you.

# Type matching
Six types: Ember, Tide, Bloom, Stone, Spark and Gloom. A good match does
double damage, a bad one does half, and using a move of your own type adds
a quarter on top. One well-chosen move beats three wrong ones.

# Catching
Lures work best on beasts that are hurt and low level — wear one down
first. A healthy high-level beast will almost always shake free.

# Losing
If your whole team goes down you wake at camp, patched up, a hundred coins
lighter. Nothing else is lost.

# Controls
  number  choose from any menu
  enter   accept the value in brackets at a prompt
