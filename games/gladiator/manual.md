# How to play
Ten opponents stand between you and the wooden sword that buys your freedom.

# The sand
Attack costs stamina. Defend restores it and raises your guard, which
subtracts from the next blow. Heavy hits hard but misses often and leaves
you open.

Feint and taunt work the crowd. Crowd favour is not decoration: reach
seventy and the editor may spare you the first time you would have died.

# Between bouts
Rest to heal, train to raise your maximum health, or oil up to win the
crowd back. Denarii come from winning, and from how loud the stands were.

# Controls
  a  attack      h  heavy       d  defend
  f  feint       t  taunt       q  yield the bout
